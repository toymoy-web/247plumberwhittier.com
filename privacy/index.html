Summary

This website does not have contact forms, user accounts, or a checkout. The main way information reaches us is when you call the phone number shown on the site. This policy explains what is collected in that case, what third-party services load on the pages, and what rights California residents have.

Information Collected When You Call

When you call, the phone number you call from, the time and length of the call, and what you tell the dispatcher may be recorded or logged. Calls may be recorded or monitored for quality, dispatch accuracy, and call tracking. You will normally hear a notice at the start of the call.

The call may be answered by a third-party dispatch service that connects you with an independent local plumbing professional. That service and the professional receive the details you provide so they can respond to your request.

Information Collected By This Website

We do not ask you to enter personal information on this website. Like most websites, the servers that deliver the pages may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested.

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and an optional map from Google Maps. Those requests go to Google and may include your IP address and browser details, subject to Google's own privacy policy. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, this policy will be updated first.

How Information Is Used

Information from calls is used to respond to your request, route you to a professional, measure whether the phone line works, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell the content of calls for unrelated marketing.

Email you send to contact@247plumberwhittier.com is used to answer you and is kept only as long as needed for that purpose and for records.

Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know what personal information is collected about you. You may also ask us to delete it, correct it, or stop any sale or sharing of it, subject to legal exceptions. You will not be treated differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email contact@247plumberwhittier.com and include the phone number you called from and the approximate date of the call. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children, Security, And Changes

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. We take reasonable steps to protect information, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@247plumberwhittier.com or by mail to 227 2nd St, Suite 8, Whittier, CA. See also our terms of use and contact page.
