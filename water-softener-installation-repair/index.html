White Crust On Faucets And Spotty Dishes?

A chalky crust around faucets, cloudy glassware, soap that will not lather, and a water heater that rumbles are the everyday signs of hard water. Water softener installation and repair in Whittier addresses the cause by removing the dissolved calcium and magnesium before they reach your pipes and appliances.

Hardness varies by street because Whittier is served by several providers, including City of Whittier Water, Suburban Water Systems, San Gabriel Valley Water Company, and Orchard Dale Water District. Each publishes an annual water quality report, and your own provider's number is the one to use.

Find Your Hardness Before You Buy

Sizing a softener starts with a number, not a guess.

  • Look up your provider's latest water quality report and find the hardness reading, listed in grains per gallon or milligrams per liter.
  • Convert if needed: one grain per gallon equals about 17.1 milligrams per liter.
  • Count the people in the household, since daily water use drives how often the unit regenerates.
  • Test at your own tap with an inexpensive strip if you want to confirm, because blended supplies can change.
  • Share the reading with the technician. The sizing, salt use, and regeneration schedule all follow from it.
  • Look for NSF/ANSI 44 certification on ion exchange softeners, the published standard for their performance claims.

Why A Softener Stops Softening

Salt bridge

A hard crust in the salt tank keeps salt from dissolving and the resin from regenerating.

Clogged injector or screen

Debris in the brine path starves the resin of the brine it needs.

Control head failure

A worn valve or a dead timer stops the regeneration cycle.

Exhausted resin

Resin beads wear out over many years and lose capacity.

Local Rules On Salt And Discharge

A regenerating softener sends brine to the drain. Some California communities restrict salt-regenerating units because of salt in wastewater, so the technician should confirm local rules before installing one. If a restriction applies, salt-free options or off-site exchange tanks may be the practical route.

Also keep sodium in perspective. A softened supply adds a small amount of sodium to drinking water, and people on sodium-restricted diets should ask a physician about it. We do not make health claims here.

What Soft Water Protects

Scale shortens the life of anything that heats water. A new water heater lasts longer with softened supply, and owners of tankless water heaters often find flushes less frequent. Treating hard water can also slow scale in the pipes that repiping would replace.

If taste or chlorine odor is the real complaint, water filtration installation is the better tool, since a softener does not filter. We install and repair softeners in La Mirada and across Whittier.
