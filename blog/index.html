Before You Act On Any Guide

General information helps, but your house is specific. A few habits keep you safe.

  • Treat gas smells, sparking, and active flooding as emergencies. Leave, shut off the source if it is safe, and call for help first.
  • Confirm local rules with your city or water provider, since permits, boundaries, and rebates differ.
  • Check any contractor's license on the state licensing board's website before work begins.
  • Get the price and the scope in writing before you agree to a repair.
  • Photograph damage and keep receipts, because insurers and buyers both ask for them.
