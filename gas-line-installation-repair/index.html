What To Do If You Smell Gas

Safety steps for a gas leak start with your gas utility, not a plumber.

  • Leave the house immediately and take everyone with you, including pets.
  • Do not turn lights or switches on or off, use a lighter, or start a car in the garage.
  • From a safe distance outside, call your gas utility's emergency line or 911.
  • Do not go back inside until the utility says it is safe.
  • After the utility has cleared the house, call us to find and repair the cause.

What Gas Line Work Includes In Whittier

Gas line installation and repair in Whittier covers new lines for ranges, dryers, fireplaces, water heaters, and outdoor kitchens, plus repairs to aging or damaged piping. Most homes here are heated and cooked with utility gas, so many houses already have a network of gas lines of various ages.

Gas work is regulated for good reason. The California Plumbing Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), governs gas piping. Piping must be sized for the connected appliances, supported, protected from damage, tested, and inspected. A line that looks fine but was never tested is a hazard.

Why Quake History Matters For Gas Lines

The October 1, 1987 Whittier Narrows earthquake is a reminder that gas fires and leaks are a risk after strong shaking. That is why flexible connectors, secure water heater straps, and automatic shutoff valves get attention in Southern California.

Some jurisdictions require seismic shutoff valves in certain situations, such as new construction or major renovation. Ask your city's building department what applies to your address, and ask the technician to confirm it in the quote.

Signs A Gas Line Needs Attention

Rotten-egg odor

Utility gas has an added odor so leaks are noticeable. Treat any smell as an emergency.

Hissing near a pipe or appliance

A sound near a connection can indicate escaping gas.

Corroded or flaking pipe

Rust on exposed steel pipe or fittings suggests the line is deteriorating.

Pilot lights that keep going out

Weak flames can signal low pressure or a failing connection.
