A Subdivider's City With A Space Program Past

Downey incorporated on December 17, 1956. It is named for Governor John G. Downey, an Irish-born Democrat who pioneered the modern subdivision in the area around 1865.

North American Aviation, later Rockwell and then Boeing, took over a former aircraft plant here, and in 1961 the facilities passed from the Air Force to NASA. Apollo systems and the Space Shuttle were developed on that site, and the Columbia Memorial Space Center now stands on part of it.

Calls We Hear Most Often In Downey

A dense city produces a steady mix of problems.

Water heater leaks

A tank that weeps at the base needs replacement, and a flooded closet or garage is the usual reason for the call.

Main line backups

Roots and aging pipe cause the same stoppage to return after a basic cleaning.

Low pressure

Corroded supply lines or a failing regulator show up as weak showers and sputtering faucets.

Fixture leaks

Angle stops, toilets, and kitchen faucets fail often in older homes and rented units.

Owning Versus Renting In A Half-Rented City

Downey housing splits about evenly, with roughly half of homes owner-occupied and half rented. That split shapes who decides what.

Owner-occupied home

  • The owner approves repairs and holds permits.
  • A camera look at the sewer lateral is a smart step before a major repair.
  • Replacing a tank before it fails costs less than an emergency swap.
  • Records of work help at resale.

Rented home or unit

  • The tenant stops the water and reports the problem.
  • The landlord approves the repair in most cases.
  • A plumber can confirm the cause for the landlord.
  • Shared lines may affect more than one unit.

What To Tell The Dispatcher From A Downey Address

A few details let the dispatcher send the right technician with the right parts.

  • Give the street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Say if the home is owned, rented, or part of a multi-unit building.
  • Describe where the water is and whether you have shut off the main valve.
  • Mention the age of the house if you know it, because older lines call for different parts.
  • Say if you have already tried a plunger or a drain product, since that affects how the line is cleared.

If You Have A Burst Pipe Or Active Leak In Downey

In a dense city, water spreads to neighbors fast, so the first minutes count.

Shut off the main water valve

Turn the main valve clockwise at the meter or the front wall. In a multi-unit building, find the valve for your unit.

Kill power to wet areas

If water reached outlets or the garage water heater, switch off those breakers before you touch anything.

Warn the neighbors below or beside you

In a shared building, tell the units below and next to you, since water travels along walls and floors.

Call the 24/7 line

Give the address, the cross street, and whether you own or rent so the dispatcher can plan the visit.
