What To Do In An Emergency, And What Can Wait

An emergency is anything that cannot wait for a scheduled visit. Water you cannot stop, sewage backing into the house, a leaking water heater, and a smell of gas all qualify. Planned work includes replacements, remodels, filtration, softener installs, and inspections, and those get a free estimate.

The difference affects price and speed. Emergency work is quoted on the call before anyone starts, and planned work is quoted after a look at the job. If you are unsure which category you are in, call and ask. The dispatcher will tell you honestly if the problem can wait until morning.

Plumbing work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), whichever category you land in. Keep the main water shutoff location in mind. Knowing where it is turns many emergencies into manageable repairs, and the burst pipe page walks through the first five minutes in detail.
